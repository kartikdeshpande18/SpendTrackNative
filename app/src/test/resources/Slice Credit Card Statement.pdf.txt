Payment obligations
The Total Amount Due (TAD) is the full
outstanding balance on your card,
including all transactions, fees, and
charges.Please ensure that at least the
Minimum Amount Due (MAD) is paid by
the due date. Failure to do so within
three days of the due date will result in
reporting of default to credit bureaus.
For details on how your MAD is
calculated, please refer to the slice
credit card MITC (Most Important Terms
and Conditions) available on slice app or
website (https://slice.bank.in/cc-mitc/).
Credit card security

event of loss, theft, or any suspicious
activity, contact our customer support
through the slice app or call on 080
4832 9999.

Glossary
Refunds & repayments
All amounts credited to your credit card
balance during this statement’s billing
cycle, including refunds, reversals,
repayments, waivers, and any cashback.
Interest
If any portion from the total bill due
remains unpaid after the payment due
date, interest or finance charges may
apply as per the average daily reducing
balance method. Paying only the
minimum amount each month may
result in long-term debt with
compounded interest and loss of
interest-free credit period benefits.
Surcharge
Fee applicable on specific spend
categories such as rent, education,
online gaming or wallet loads. More
details can be found in slice credit card
MITC.
Flat fee
Fee for processing bank transfers from
your slice credit card cash limit to your
savings account.
Glossary
Late fee
Fee charged when the minimum due
amount isn’t paid by the due date. The
fee is applied as per the structured
schedule based on your outstanding
balance.
Foreclosure charge
Fee charged to facilitate early closure of
an active sliced transaction. For details
on how the foreclosure charge is
calculated, refer to the slicing facility
terms or the slice UPI credit card MITC.
Ongoing EMIs
Monthly installment payments due for
active sliced transactions or any active
EMIs on your credit card.
Carry forward
Any outstanding balance from your
previous month's statement that
remains unpaid is carried forward.
